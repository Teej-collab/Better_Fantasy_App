# THE WEEKEND — Creator & Podcast Strategy

**Status:** Proposal for review.

---

## Why micro over macro, at this stage

A mega-influencer or a top-10 fantasy podcast sending a large audience at THE WEEKEND before the multi-league frontend is finished (`00_Product_Marketing_Audit.md`) would be actively harmful — a flood of new leagues hitting an app that can't yet reliably serve a second league produces a wave of bad first impressions that no amount of later polish fully undoes. Micro-creators solve this: smaller audiences, closer relationships, and — critically — the kind of creator whose audience genuinely trusts a personal recommendation rather than tuning it out as an ad read.

## Who to target

| Tier | Who | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro creators (highest priority)** | Fantasy TikTok/YouTube creators with roughly 1K-50K followers, especially ones who run their own home league on camera | Highest trust-per-follower ratio; genuinely reachable for a small team; audience overlaps almost exactly with `02_Target_Audience.md`'s commissioner/engaged-player persona |
| **Small-to-mid fantasy podcasts** | Shows with a real, named host and a loyal (if not huge) listener base, especially ones that discuss league management/commissioner topics | Podcast audiences over-index on being commissioners themselves (they're engaged enough to seek out fantasy content beyond their own league) |
| **Commissioners with an audience** | A commissioner of a well-known or content-documented league (a league with its own Twitter account, a YouTube recap series, etc.) | This person is simultaneously the primary customer *and* a distribution channel — a uniquely efficient target |
| **Fantasy Discord community owners/mods** | Runs or moderates a mid-size fantasy Discord | Direct access to a concentrated, high-intent audience; often under-monetized and receptive to a genuine, low-pressure offer |
| **Deprioritized for now: mega-influencers / top-tier podcasts** | 100K+ audiences, major branded shows | Revisit only after Phase 5 (`04_12_Month_Strategy.md`) once the product has proven it can absorb a real acquisition spike |

## Outreach strategy

**Who to contact:** Start from real, already-observed engagement — creators who've been genuinely interacted with via the community strategy (`09_Community_Strategy.md`), not cold lists. A warm "I've been in your comments for weeks and built something I think your audience would actually use" outperforms a cold pitch every time at this scale.

**What to say:** Be specific and honest, not a generic partnership pitch. Lead with what's real and differentiated (AI recaps/draft grades, power rankings, the founder story) and be upfront about what isn't built yet. Offer them something concrete to try firsthand — their own free league on THE WEEKEND — before asking for anything.

**What to offer:**
- Early/free access with direct founder support (genuinely valuable to a creator who wants to be first to cover something).
- A "Founding Creator" recognition parallel to the Founding Leagues program (`15_2027_Launch_Campaign.md`), if their own league runs on the platform.
- Creative input — a small creator whose feedback visibly shapes a real feature (and who can then say so) gets a more durable, credible story than a paid placement.
- A genuine affiliate/referral credit tied to the referral program in `08_Referral_Growth.md`, once that program exists — commission structured around real league activation, not clicks.

**What NOT to offer:**
- Flat cash payment for a promotional post, especially pre-launch-campaign — this is both a poor use of a near-$0 budget (`14_Metrics.md`) and produces the least-trusted kind of content (an obvious ad read) for an audience this skeptical of platform switches.
- Exclusivity demands or long-term contracts — asking a small creator to commit exclusively to an unproven platform is an unreasonable ask at this stage and will get declined by exactly the creators worth having.
- Access to other users' or leagues' private data for "content" — a hard line given the app's own documented data-isolation security work (`Documentation/Competitive_Audit`, Section 20).

## How to measure success

- **Not** follower counts or impressions. Track (per `14_Metrics.md`) the number of leagues that can be attributed to a specific creator relationship (via referral code or direct "how did you hear about us" at signup), and whether those leagues actually activate (complete a draft), not just sign up.
- A single micro-creator whose own league runs the whole season on THE WEEKEND and who mentions it organically over months is worth more than ten one-off shout-outs — optimize outreach for depth of relationship over breadth of reach.
