# THE WEEKEND — Commissioner Acquisition

**Status:** Proposal for review. This is the single most important document in this folder — see `02_Target_Audience.md` for why the commissioner is the real unit of growth.

---

## The real objection, stated honestly

Every commissioner evaluating a switch is running the same private calculation: *"Is this app good enough to be worth the political cost of asking 9-13 people to change what they're used to?"* That's not a feature question, it's a trust and effort question. The tactics below are built around lowering the effort and raising the trust, in that order — a feature list does neither on its own.

## Naming the friction directly

| Objection | Response strategy |
|---|---|
| "I don't want to make my league learn a new app." | The self-serve join flow already requires nothing more than an invite code and an email/password signup — no app install required (it's a PWA, installable later, not required upfront). The commissioner toolkit (below) gives the commissioner a one-message invite script so *they* don't have to explain anything. |
| "I don't want to move everyone's teams." | Be honest about what's true today: THE WEEKEND does not import rosters/history from ESPN/Yahoo/Sleeper for a brand-new self-serve league (that pathway only exists for the founder's own legacy league). Don't claim otherwise. Position the switch as best-timed **between seasons**, at a fresh draft, not mid-season — this is also why the Phase 5 acquisition window (`04_12_Month_Strategy.md`) is pre-draft, not mid-season. |
| "My league already uses ESPN / Sleeper." | Don't ask them to fully commit first. The lowest-friction entry point is "run your 2027 draft on THE WEEKEND as an experiment, keep ESPN/Sleeper as backup" — reduces the ask from "abandon your platform" to "try the next one." |
| "Why should I bother?" | This is where `01_Brand_Positioning.md`'s commissioner pitch and the recurring content (`06_Content_Pillars.md`, Commissioner Problems series) earn their keep — the answer has to already be top-of-mind before the ask happens, not invented in the moment. |

## Commissioner landing page strategy

A dedicated page (not the general homepage) speaking directly to the commissioner persona from `02_Target_Audience.md`. Contents: the commissioner-focused pitch (`01_Brand_Positioning.md` Section 6), a short list of real, checkable commissioner tools (trade veto, force-edit, league polls, granular scoring rules — all confirmed real per the competitive audit), an honest "what THE WEEKEND doesn't do yet" note (auction drafts, multi-team trades — undercutting this proactively builds more trust than hiding it), and a single clear CTA: "Create your league free." This page does not exist yet — it is a P0 product-marketing dependency, see `17_Growth_Roadmap.md`.

## Commissioner onboarding

A distinct first-run flow (not the generic signup) that walks a commissioner through, in order: name the league → set scoring/roster rules (with sensible defaults pre-filled, since most commissioners copy their old league's settings) → generate the invite link/code → get a copy-pasteable invite message (see toolkit below) → schedule the draft. Every step should be completable in under 10 minutes — this is a testable, trackable metric (`13_Growth_Funnel.md`).

## League migration strategy

Be explicit that this is a **new-season migration, not a mid-season one.** The realistic path is: commissioner creates the league on THE WEEKEND in the offseason, runs the 2027 draft there, and only fully retires the old platform once the league has a season of confidence. Do not build or promise an automated ESPN/Sleeper/Yahoo import for a brand-new self-serve league unless product work confirms it — check `17_Growth_Roadmap.md` P2 for whether this becomes worth building once there's real commissioner demand for it.

## Commissioner referral program

Covered in full in `08_Referral_Growth.md`; the commissioner-specific angle is that rewards should scale with **successful league activation** (a league that actually drafted and played, not just signed up) — rewarding a commissioner for a friend's league that never activated teaches the wrong behavior.

## Commissioner rewards

Recognition-based, not cash-based (see budget discipline in `14_Metrics.md`): a "Founding Commissioner" badge visible in the league's own app, custom league branding/theme options unlocked early, and priority support/direct access to the founder during the season (a genuinely valuable, low-cost reward for a small team to offer at low volume).

## Shareable league invitations

The current invite-code flow works but is bare (a code and a copy button, per `00_Product_Marketing_Audit.md`). It should become a shareable, branded invite — a short message pre-written for the commissioner to paste into their existing group chat, plus (once built, see `17_Growth_Roadmap.md`) a simple branded invite-link preview card. This is a product change, not a marketing one, but it's the single highest-leverage product change for this specific document's strategy to work.

## Commissioner toolkit

A one-page (eventually PDF or in-app) kit for a commissioner who's decided to try it: the copy-paste invite message, a "what to expect" one-pager for the league (so the commissioner isn't fielding every question personally), and a short list of what's different from ESPN/Yahoo/Sleeper (so questions get answered before they're asked).

## Commissioner social content

The "Commissioner Problems" and "Commissioner Content" material from `06_Content_Pillars.md` is the top-of-funnel version of this whole document — it exists so that by the time a commissioner lands on the commissioner page above, they already have context.

## Commissioner testimonials & case studies

Do not fabricate these. They become available starting with the Founding Leagues cohort in Phase 2 (`04_12_Month_Strategy.md`) and should be built from real quotes and real screenshots, published as short profiles (the "Founding League Spotlight" series in `06_Content_Pillars.md`) — the first real case study is realistically available no earlier than Q1 2027, once a Founding League has played a real stretch of games.

## The sequencing that makes this document honest

None of the above should run as an active acquisition push before the multi-league frontend is finished (`00_Product_Marketing_Audit.md`, `17_Growth_Roadmap.md` P0) — a commissioner acquisition campaign pointed at a product that can't yet reliably serve a second league would spend the program's only asset, trust, on a broken first impression.
