# THE WEEKEND — Metrics, Growth Scenarios & Budget

**Status:** Proposal for review. All targets below are explicitly labeled estimates with stated assumptions, not commitments — per the brief's instruction not to manufacture false certainty.

---

## 1. The metric that matters most

**Number of active leagues.** Not followers, not likes, not views. A league is "active" when it has completed a draft and set at least one lineup in the current season. One commissioner acquiring one active league is worth more than a thousand passive social followers — this is the entire thesis of `02_Target_Audience.md` and should be the north-star metric on any dashboard built for this strategy.

## 2. The full metrics set

**Top of funnel:** website visitors, signups, activated users (completed onboarding + joined/created a league).
**League funnel:** leagues created, leagues joined, invites sent, invite acceptance rate, league activation rate (% of created leagues that complete a real draft).
**Commissioners:** commissioners acquired (distinct from general signups — a commissioner is whoever created a league), cost per acquired league (once any paid spend exists), cost per acquired user.
**Draft & season health:** draft completion rate, Week 1 lineup-set rate, Week 4 retention, full-season retention.
**Growth loop:** referral rate (referred leagues per active league), organic vs. paid vs. referral acquisition mix.
**Trailing/lagging (secondary — track but don't optimize for):** social followers/engagement, content views, email open rates.

## 3. Growth scenarios — 3/6/9/12 months from launch of the real acquisition push (Phase 4-5, roughly May 2027)

These are estimates for the period after the product is actually ready to acquire leagues at volume, **not** from today (September 2026) — running the clock from today would misrepresent months spent on product-readiness work (Phases 1-3) as growth-stalled months, when they're deliberate.

| Scenario | +3 months (Aug 2027) | +6 months (Nov 2027) | +9 months (Feb 2028) | +12 months (May 2028) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** | 15-25 active leagues, ~200-350 users | 25-40 leagues | 30-50 leagues | 40-70 leagues |
| **Base case** | 40-75 active leagues, ~500-1,000 users | 75-125 leagues | 100-175 leagues | 150-250 leagues |
| **Aggressive** | 100-200 active leagues (a genuine viral/creator moment) | 200-350 leagues | 300-500 leagues | 500-900 leagues |

**Stated assumptions behind every scenario:** the multi-league frontend (`17_Growth_Roadmap.md` P0) ships and is stable before May 2027; at least one of the commissioner-acquisition or creator-outreach channels produces real results (not zero); the product doesn't suffer a reputation-damaging failure during a live draft or Sunday (the single biggest downside risk named in `00_Product_Marketing_Audit.md`). **What would need to be true for the aggressive case:** a genuine organic moment (a Reddit post or creator video that meaningfully outperforms expectations) plus a functioning referral loop actually compounding — i.e., an aggressive outcome requires something outside this plan's direct control, not just better execution of it. Treat the base case as the actual planning number.

**The brief's stated target — "multiple new leagues using the platform" entering the 2027 season** — is achieved even by the conservative scenario. The stretch goal of "hundreds or thousands of leagues" (Part 30) is a multi-year outcome, not a 12-month one, and pretending otherwise would violate the brief's own instruction not to invent false certainty.

## 4. Budget tiers

| Tier | What it buys | What changes vs. the tier below |
|---|---|---|
| **$0/month** | Reddit/Discord/community participation, organic social content, SEO content, direct creator outreach (free/reciprocal offers only), email/push (once built — infrastructure cost is developer time, not spend) | This is the default and realistic tier through most of Phase 1-3; everything in this strategy through March 2027 should assume $0 |
| **$250/month** | A domain + basic paid tooling (email delivery service, a lightweight analytics/attribution tool if the built-in analytics taxonomy needs extending), very small boosted-post spend on the highest-performing organic content only | First real infrastructure spend; still no broad paid acquisition |
| **$500/month** | The above, plus a small, targeted paid spend testing Reddit ads or a boosted post in a specific fantasy-adjacent audience segment during Phase 5 (June-August 2027 only, not year-round), plus a modest budget for a micro-creator sponsorship (not a cash-for-post arrangement — see `10_Creator_Strategy.md` for why that's still the wrong instrument even with a budget) | First real paid acquisition testing, tightly scoped to the highest-leverage window |
| **$1,000/month** | The above, plus 1-2 concurrent micro-creator partnerships with modest paid components, plus paid promotion of the pre-season 2027 campaign (`15_2027_Launch_Campaign.md`) across the peak June-August window | Enough to meaningfully amplify Phase 5, still nowhere near enough for broad-audience paid social — stay disciplined about channel selection at this tier, not just spend more everywhere |

**The rule governing all four tiers:** spend follows proof, not hope. No tier above $0 should activate before the $0 tier has demonstrated that the funnel in `13_Growth_Funnel.md` actually converts — paying to send more traffic into a leaky, unproven funnel just produces a bigger, more expensive leak.

## 5. What NOT to track as success

Follower counts, like counts, and raw video view counts are explicitly excluded as primary success metrics per the brief. They remain useful as secondary, directional signals (a video that dramatically outperforms others is worth understanding), but no decision in this strategy should be made to optimize them directly.
