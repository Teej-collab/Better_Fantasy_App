# THE WEEKEND — Content Pillars & Recurring Series

**Status:** Proposal for review. Every pillar and series below is chosen because it can be substantially generated from real product data (the AI recap engine, the awards/power-ranking engine, real draft results) — this is deliberate, see `16_Weekly_Execution_System.md` on why a small team should build content that the product itself produces, not content that has to be invented from scratch every week.

---

## Content pillars

### Pillar 1: League Culture & Drama
**Purpose:** Prove the "your league already has a personality, THE WEEKEND gives it a home" positioning with real, specific examples. **Audience:** Commissioners and players, primary + secondary. **Example posts:** a real (anonymized/consented) AI recap excerpt roasting a bad lineup decision; a rivalry stat card. **Short-form concepts:** "read the AI recap out loud" reaction format. **Recurring series:** Bench Crime of the Week, League Villain of the Week (Section "Recurring Series" below). **CTA:** "this is what your league's app should be doing."

### Pillar 2: Commissioner Content
**Purpose:** Speak directly to the highest-leverage audience (`02_Target_Audience.md`). **Audience:** Commissioners specifically. **Example posts:** "things ESPN won't tell you as a commissioner" style tips that are true and useful even to a non-switcher (builds trust before the ask). **Short-form concepts:** a 30-second walkthrough of a commissioner tool (trade veto, force-edit, league polls). **Recurring series:** Commissioner Problems (Section below). **CTA:** commissioner landing page link (`07_Commissioner_Acquisition.md`).

### Pillar 3: Draft Content
**Purpose:** Draft night is the single highest-emotion, highest-shareability moment in fantasy football, and THE WEEKEND has a genuinely differentiated feature here (AI draft grades) with no confirmed native equivalent on Sleeper/ESPN/Yahoo. **Audience:** Both. **Example posts:** real draft-grade cards, "best value pick" call-outs. **Short-form concepts:** a draft-grade reveal reaction format. **Recurring series:** Draft Grade Roasts. **CTA:** "get your league's draft graded — free."

### Pillar 4: Sunday / Game-Day Content
**Purpose:** Ride the natural weekly spike in fantasy attention. **Audience:** Both. **Example posts:** live win-probability swings, a Gamecast screenshot of a big red-zone moment. **Short-form concepts:** "this app updates faster than my group chat" format. **Recurring series:** Sunday Meltdown (Section below). **CTA:** none needed — this pillar's job is engagement/awareness, not conversion.

### Pillar 5: THE WEEKEND Features (Product-as-content)
**Purpose:** Explain real, specific, currently-true features without sounding like a demo video. **Audience:** Commissioners evaluating a switch. **Example posts:** "here's what our lineup lock actually looks like" (a real, checkable claim per `01_Brand_Positioning.md`'s voice rule). **Short-form concepts:** 15-second feature clips. **Recurring series:** none dedicated — folded into other series as a closing beat. **CTA:** direct, "try it free."

### Pillar 6: Building in Public
**Purpose:** The founder-story audience (`02_Target_Audience.md` tertiary) and general trust-building — "watch this get built" is inherently more credible than "trust our marketing." **Audience:** Tertiary + skeptical commissioners who want proof of active maintenance. **Example posts:** a real commit message turned into a plain-English post ("fixed the bug where you could start a player after his game ended"). **Short-form concepts:** screen-recorded before/after of a bug fix. **Recurring series:** Weekly Ship Log (Section below). **CTA:** none — credibility-building, not conversion.

## Recurring series

1. **Bench Crime of the Week** — *Concept:* the AI recap engine already computes a "bench crime" (highest-scoring player left on a bench) per league per week; turn the real output into a short clip. *Format:* 15-30s, screenshot + voiceover of the AI recap line. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels/Shorts. *Frequency:* weekly, in-season. *Why shared:* the "victim" almost always shares it themselves to either laugh it off or defend themselves — this is real, product-native virality, not manufactured. *Connects back:* every clip is visibly generated by the product, not edited by hand.

2. **League Villain of the Week** — *Concept:* built from real weekly awards (biggest meltdown, worst trade, most heartless waiver claim). *Format:* short-form with a "villain card" graphic. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels + Instagram carousel. *Frequency:* weekly. *Why shared:* the target either owns it (fun) or contests it (engagement) — both outcomes drive sharing. *Connects back:* real awards-engine output, unique to THE WEEKEND among the three incumbents per the competitive audit.

3. **Draft Grade Roasts** — *Concept:* real AI-generated draft grades, presented as a reaction format. *Format:* 30-60s. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels, timed to real draft season (Aug-Sept). *Frequency:* daily during peak draft season only. *Why shared:* every drafter wants to see (and argue with) their own grade. *Connects back:* directly promotes the free-to-try draft-grade feature.

4. **Commissioner Problems** — *Concept:* a recurring bit about real commissioner headaches (the guy who won't set his lineup, the trade that should never have been approved) paired with the specific tool that solves it. *Format:* 20-30s skit or voiceover-over-screen-recording. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels + Reddit text version. *Frequency:* weekly. *Why shared:* every commissioner has lived this exact story. *Connects back:* directly to commissioner tools, feeding `07_Commissioner_Acquisition.md`.

5. **Sunday Meltdown** — *Concept:* a live, real-time capture of the single biggest win-probability swing of the week, pulled from Gamecast. *Format:* screen recording + reaction. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels, posted same-day. *Frequency:* weekly, in-season, Sunday evening. *Why shared:* real drama is inherently more shareable than staged content. *Connects back:* showcases the live Gamecast feature directly.

6. **Weekly Ship Log** — *Concept:* a plain-English translation of the week's real commits/fixes. *Format:* short text post (X) + optional 30s "before/after" clip for a visual fix. *Platform:* X primarily, Reddit occasionally. *Frequency:* weekly, year-round. *Why shared:* the build-in-public audience reliably engages with visible momentum. *Connects back:* trust and credibility, the foundation the whole strategy depends on per `00_Product_Marketing_Audit.md`.

7. **Power Rankings Reveal** — *Concept:* real weekly power-ranking output, presented as a countdown graphic. *Format:* Instagram carousel / short-form countdown. *Platform:* Instagram + TikTok. *Frequency:* weekly, in-season. *Why shared:* teams near the bottom get defensive, teams near the top brag — both drive engagement within the league's own circle. *Connects back:* a real, documented-formula feature the competitive audit found no confirmed native equivalent for on any competitor.

8. **Who Got Screwed?** — *Concept:* built from real trade-veto or close-matchup data — a weekly "closest call" or "most controversial ruling" breakdown. *Format:* 30-45s narrated. *Platform:* TikTok/Reels. *Frequency:* biweekly (not every week has a real one — never manufacture drama that isn't real). *Connects back:* commissioner trade-review tooling.

9. **Record Book Update** — *Concept:* whenever a real all-time record is broken (highest score, biggest blowout), post it immediately. *Format:* single graphic, real-time. *Platform:* X + Instagram story. *Frequency:* event-driven, not scheduled. *Why shared:* genuine, rare, real news within a league. *Connects back:* the all-time record-book feature.

10. **Founding League Spotlight** — *Concept:* once the Founding Leagues program (`15_2027_Launch_Campaign.md`) has real participants, profile one per installment — their league's history, why they switched, a real screenshot. *Format:* short interview-style post/carousel. *Platform:* all channels. *Frequency:* biweekly starting Phase 4 (`04_12_Month_Strategy.md`). *Why shared:* the commissioner being featured shares it to their own league. *Connects back:* direct social proof for the commissioner-acquisition funnel.

## The constraint that makes this system work

Series 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, and 9 are **generated substantially from real product output**, not hand-written from scratch — this is what makes a 3x/week cadence achievable for a small team (see `16_Weekly_Execution_System.md` for the production pipeline). Series that require pure invention (Commissioner Problems, Who Got Screwed) are deliberately fewer and lower-frequency.
