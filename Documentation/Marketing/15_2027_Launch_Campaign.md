# THE WEEKEND — Pre-Season 2027 Campaign & Founding Leagues Program

**Status:** Proposal for review.

---

## Part 1: The pre-season campaign

**Campaign name:** *"Draft Somewhere That Remembers."* (Alternate: *"Bring Your League Home."*) Both play directly off the brand positioning in `01_Brand_Positioning.md` — the pitch isn't "switch platforms," it's "your league's history deserves a real home."

**Campaign message:** "Your league already has a personality. This is the first season it gets to keep it." Consistent, single message across every channel — not a different pitch per platform.

**Landing page:** The commissioner landing page from `07_Commissioner_Acquisition.md`, with a campaign-specific hero treatment and a visible countdown to the 2027 draft window, live starting the moment Phase 4 opens (May 2027, per `04_12_Month_Strategy.md`).

**Commissioner offer:** Founding League status (Part 2 below) for any league that runs its 2027 draft on THE WEEKEND, positioned as free and permanent, not a limited trial.

**Social campaign:** The full content-pillar system (`06_Content_Pillars.md`) runs at peak frequency during this window — daily Draft Grade Roasts once real drafts start happening on the platform, Commissioner Problems content ramped in the weeks prior.

**Referral campaign:** The referral program (`08_Referral_Growth.md`) gets a campaign-specific push — every Founding League is explicitly asked, once activated, to refer one more league, with the ask embedded in their own onboarding confirmation, not left to a generic footer link.

**Creator campaign:** Micro-creator and podcast partnerships (`10_Creator_Strategy.md`) are timed to peak in the 4-6 weeks before the average fantasy draft date (mid-to-late August), matching when their audiences are actually making platform decisions.

**Email campaign:** A short, honest re-engagement sequence to anyone who signed up earlier in the year but didn't yet create/activate a league ("your league's 2027 draft is coming up — here's what's new since you looked") — see `12_Email_Strategy.md`.

**Countdown / draft-season content:** A visible, real countdown to "peak draft season," paired with content answering the actual questions a commissioner has in this window (how scoring rules transfer, how invites work, what happens if only half the league wants to switch).

**Launch timeline (within Phase 5, `04_12_Month_Strategy.md`):**
- **Early June 2027:** Campaign page goes live, Founding Leagues program opens to general applicants (not just the Phase 2 invite-only cohort).
- **June-July:** Content and creator cadence ramps; referral push begins.
- **Late July-mid August:** Peak spend window (if any budget tier above $0 is active, per `14_Metrics.md`) and peak creator activity, timed to the real distribution of draft dates.
- **Late August-mid September:** Execution mode (Phase 6) — support and monitoring take priority over new acquisition activity.

## Part 2: Founding Leagues program

**Should THE WEEKEND run this?** Yes — a time-boxed "Founding" cohort is a well-suited mechanic here specifically because the product's real strength (per `03_Competitive_Positioning.md`) is depth of care for a small number of leagues, not breadth; a founding cohort lets that be true honestly at every stage of growth rather than becoming false the moment the user base grows.

**Eligibility:** Any league that completes a real draft on THE WEEKEND before a stated cutoff (recommend: end of the 2027 draft season, mid-September 2027) — deliberately generous and simple, not an artificial-scarcity gate. The value of "Founding" status comes from being early, not from being hard to get.

**Benefits:**
- Permanent "Founding League" badge/status visible in the league's own app.
- Early access to every future feature, ahead of leagues that join later.
- Direct line to the founder for support and feature input during the season that matters most for building trust (their first).
- Locked-in free status if a paid tier is ever introduced — a credible, low-cost-today promise that rewards the leagues taking the real risk of trying an early product.
- A guaranteed spot in the "Founding League Spotlight" content series (`06_Content_Pillars.md`), if the commissioner wants it (opt-in, never assumed).

**Recognition:** Public (with consent) — the spotlight series, a visible badge, potential inclusion in future landing-page social proof once there's enough of a track record to show real testimonials (`07_Commissioner_Acquisition.md`).

**Pricing advantages:** Not applicable today (no paid tier exists, and per the competitive audit's own P4 recommendation, none should be built prematurely) — but the *promise* of a locked-in advantage if one is ever introduced is a real, low-cost incentive worth making explicitly.

**Exclusive features:** Early access, not permanently exclusive features — a founding cohort should feel privileged by timing, not by a permanently different product, which would fragment the codebase and the support burden for a small team.

**Referral benefits:** Layered directly onto the general referral program (`08_Referral_Growth.md`) — Founding Leagues are the first and most natural source of referred leagues, since they're the ones with real, positive lived experience to share.

**Long-term value:** The first cohort of Founding Leagues is the actual proof this entire strategy needs by early-mid 2027 — real testimonials, real case studies, real bug reports from configurations League #1 never exposed, and a group of genuinely invested advocates going into the highest-leverage acquisition window of the year.
