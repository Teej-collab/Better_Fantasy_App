# THE WEEKEND — Social Media Strategy

**Status:** Proposal for review. Priority order reflects a one-to-few-person team — this is a "do two things well" plan, not a "be everywhere" plan.

---

## Platform priority

| Priority | Platform | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reddit** (r/fantasyfootball + league/format-specific subs) | Highest-intent audience, zero production cost, direct commissioner reach |
| 2 | **TikTok / Instagram Reels** (shared vertical-video content) | Best format for the recurring-series content this product's data naturally produces |
| 3 | **Discord** | Community, not broadcast — see `09_Community_Strategy.md` for the full plan |
| 4 | **X (Twitter)** | Secondary, for building-in-public and fantasy-Twitter engagement |
| 5 | **YouTube Shorts** | Repurposing target for TikTok/Reels content, not a separate production line |
| Not prioritized | Facebook, Threads, standalone long-form YouTube | See "Deprioritized" below |

### 1. Reddit — the highest-leverage, lowest-cost channel

- **Purpose:** Direct reach into exactly the people who are already actively evaluating fantasy platforms and complaining about their current one — r/fantasyfootball threads about ESPN/Yahoo frustrations are a recurring, evergreen genre.
- **Audience:** Commissioners and engaged players, self-selected by being in the subreddit at all.
- **Content type:** Genuine participation first — answering "is there a good ESPN alternative" threads honestly (including naming real limitations), and only occasionally a direct post about THE WEEKEND itself, always framed as "here's what I built for my own league" (true) rather than a product pitch.
- **Posting frequency:** Daily light engagement (replies, upvotes, genuine participation) from month 1; a direct top-level post no more than 1-2x/month, timed to real news (a feature ship, the pre-season campaign, a Founding Leagues call for applicants).
- **Tone:** Founder-voice, first person, transparent about what's real and what isn't (Reddit punishes obvious marketing speak harder than any other channel).
- **Growth strategy:** Karma and trust built through genuine participation for months before any promotional post — this is not optional, it's how the platform's culture works.
- **Conversion strategy:** Every genuine, non-promotional reply that happens to be relevant is worth more than a promotional post; the actual conversion event is a commissioner DMing to ask about it, not a click-through.

### 2. TikTok / Instagram Reels — the content-pillar delivery channel

- **Purpose:** Distribute the recurring series in `06_Content_Pillars.md` in the format fantasy football audiences actually consume them in (see any existing fantasy-football TikTok creator's engagement — it's overwhelmingly short-form).
- **Audience:** Broader fantasy-engaged audience, secondary customer from `02_Target_Audience.md` — top-of-funnel awareness, not direct commissioner conversion.
- **Content type:** The recurring series (Section 3, `06_Content_Pillars.md`) — mostly built from real product data (an actual bench-crime award, an actual AI recap line, an actual power-ranking swing), captioned/voiced over.
- **Posting frequency:** 3x/week once the content-production system (`16_Weekly_Execution_System.md`) is running; do not start this before Phase 3 (`04_12_Month_Strategy.md`) — there's no point building a content cadence before there's a product worth funneling people to.
- **Tone:** Funny first, product-aware second — the product should appear as the source of the content ("this is a real award THE WEEKEND generated for a real league"), not as the subject of every video.
- **Growth strategy:** Post identical or near-identical cuts to both platforms simultaneously (same clip, native captions) — do not treat them as separate production lines.
- **Conversion strategy:** Bio link to the landing page; a periodic (not every video) direct CTA ("this is free, link in bio").

### 3. Discord — see `09_Community_Strategy.md` for full detail; summarized here for platform-priority context

- **Purpose:** Where the actual community lives — not a broadcast channel, a place fantasy commissioners already spend time and where THE WEEKEND can be a genuine participant.

### 4. X (Twitter) — secondary, low-cost maintenance channel

- **Purpose:** Building-in-public updates, engaging with fantasy-Twitter and other indie builders; a support channel, not a growth engine.
- **Audience:** Overlaps with the tertiary "builder-in-public" audience from `02_Target_Audience.md`, plus fantasy-content Twitter.
- **Content type:** Founder-voice build updates, screenshots of new features, occasional repost/engagement with fantasy-football discourse.
- **Posting frequency:** 3-5x/week, low production cost (text + screenshot, not edited video).
- **Tone:** Direct, unpolished, "here's what I shipped this week."
- **Growth strategy:** Engagement in replies to bigger fantasy accounts and other build-in-public accounts, not follower-count chasing.
- **Conversion strategy:** Secondary; this channel's job is credibility and archive, not direct signups.

### 5. YouTube Shorts — pure repurposing target

- **Purpose:** Free additional distribution for content already produced for TikTok/Reels — same asset, uploaded natively (not embedded/watermarked) to Shorts.
- **Effort:** Near-zero incremental cost once the TikTok/Reels pipeline exists; do not produce anything Shorts-specific.

## Deprioritized, with reasons

| Platform | Why not |
|---|---|
| Facebook | Audience skews away from the core commissioner persona; no evidence fantasy-football discovery happens there at meaningful volume for a product this early |
| Threads | Small, uncertain fantasy-sports presence; revisit only if X's utility degrades |
| Standalone long-form YouTube | High production cost relative to a 1-few-person team's capacity; the content pillars in `06_Content_Pillars.md` are short-form-native. Revisit only if a specific long-form format (e.g., a draft-night stream) proves itself organically |
| Broad paid social ads | Wrong unit economics before the product and landing page can convert a commissioner — see `02_Target_Audience.md` and budget guidance in `14_Metrics.md` |

## The rule that governs all of it

Nothing above starts in earnest before Phase 3 of `04_12_Month_Strategy.md` (March 2027) except Reddit genuine-participation and X build-in-public, which can and should start immediately (Phase 1) because they cost almost nothing and build trust that compounds. Do not run the TikTok/Reels content machine before there's a product a viewer could actually go try.
