Direction C — Scoreboard Wall · Mobile

Four tabs instead of one screen (Wall / Video / Watch / Chat — a phone can't show them all at once) — Wall opens by default since that's the point of this direction. A small persistent picture-in-picture bubble keeps the current speaker visible no matter which tab you're on, so you never fully lose the "watch party" feeling for the scoreboard.

Interaction model: tab-switching is a real cost (one more decision than A or B), but it's the only way to give the wall real estate without either shrinking video to nothing or hiding the wall entirely. The PiP bubble is what keeps this from feeling like you left the call. Screen share gets its own fourth tab — starting a share auto-switches you there (you wouldn't start one and not look at it), with a red dot marking it live so you can find your way back after checking the wall or chat.
