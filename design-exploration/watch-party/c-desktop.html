Direction C — Scoreboard Wall · Desktop

Completely different model: instead of overlaying fantasy data on top of video, this flips the hierarchy. The wall of every live matchup IS the main screen — like a sports bar's TV wall — and the video call shrinks to a rail on the side. Built for a league that cares as much about everyone else's game as their own.

Interaction model: the call is ambient (small tiles, easy to glance at), the wall is where attention actually goes — every matchup gets equal billing, not just yours. Trades video presence for league-wide situational awareness.
